What is a QR Code Generator?

A QR Code Generator is a free online tool that converts any text, link, or information into a QR code. QR stands for Quick Response. When someone scans this code with their mobile camera, they can instantly open your link, see your text, or save your contact. You do not need to install any app or software. Just type your link, click Generate, and your QR code is ready in seconds.

How to Use QR Code Generator?

Using this tool is very easy. First, copy your link. It can be your YouTube video link, WhatsApp link, Facebook page, Instagram profile, website, or any text. Second, paste it in the box above. Third, select the size - Small, Medium, or Large. Fourth, click on Generate QR Code button. Your QR code will appear on the screen. You can download it as a PNG image and use it anywhere.

Example

For example, if you have a shop and you want customers to open your WhatsApp easily, just paste your WhatsApp link like https://wa.me/923001234567 and generate the QR code. Customers will scan it and directly open your WhatsApp. Similarly, YouTubers use QR codes on their thumbnails and posters so viewers can open their channel in one scan.
