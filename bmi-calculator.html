What is BMI Calculator?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a simple number that tells you if your weight is healthy according to your height. Doctors all over the world use BMI to check if a person is underweight, normal, overweight, or obese. Our free BMI Calculator does this calculation for you in seconds. You just need to enter your weight and height, and it will show your BMI and your category.

How to Use BMI Calculator?

Using this tool is very easy. First, enter your weight in kilograms. For example, if your weight is 65 kg, type 65. Second, enter your height in centimeters. For example, if your height is 170 cm, type 170. Third, click on Calculate BMI button. Your BMI result will appear instantly. You will also see your category - Underweight, Normal, Overweight, or Obese.

Example

Let us understand with an example. Ahmed weighs 70 kg and his height is 175 cm. When he enters these values, his BMI comes to 22.9. This means Ahmed is in the Normal category. This is a healthy range. Another example, Sara weighs 85 kg and her height is 160 cm. Her BMI comes to 33.2. This means she is in the Obese category and she should consult a doctor and start healthy eating and exercise.

BMI Chart and Meaning

If your BMI is below 18.5, you are underweight. You need to eat healthy food and gain some weight. If your BMI is between 18.5 and 24.9, you are normal. Congratulations, keep your healthy lifestyle. If your BMI is between 25 and 29.9, you are overweight. You should do daily walk and control your diet. If your BMI is 30 or above, you are obese. This can cause sugar, blood pressure, and heart problems, so you should take advice from a doctor. Remember, BMI is a guideline, not a full medical report. For children, pregnant women, and athletes, BMI may be different. Always consult a doctor for complete advice.
