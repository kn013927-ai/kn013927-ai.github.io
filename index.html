Understanding Your Age Calculation

This calculator works out your age from your date of birth to today. It shows your age in completed years, months, and days.

How to Use It

Select your date of birth, then click the Calculate Age button. Check that the date and year are correct before calculating.

Example

If your date of birth is 1 January 2000, the calculator uses today's date to work out how many years, months, and days have passed.

About the Result

The result is an age calculation, not an official document. For forms or legal purposes, follow the instructions of the organization requesting your age.

Unit Converter

How to Use the Unit Converter

Enter a number in the input box. Select the type of conversion you want from the dropdown menu, then click the Convert button to see the result.

Example 1: Enter 1 and select Kilometers to Miles. The result will be approximately 0.621 miles.

Example 2: Enter 1 and select Kilograms to Pounds. The result will be approximately 2.205 pounds.
